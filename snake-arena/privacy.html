SNAKE ARENA / PRIVACY

Privacy, plainly stated.

Snake Arena is a static browser game. We do not require an account and do not collect names, email addresses, or payment details.

Local storage

Your best score is saved in your browser's local storage so it remains available on this device. You can clear it through your browser settings.

Advertising and sponsored links

Game screens contain labeled static advertising placeholders and one optional Sponsored Offers link. Selecting that ordinary link opens a third-party sponsored offer in a new tab. It is never opened automatically, preloaded, refreshed, rotated, or connected to gameplay, rewards, scores, timers, or leaderboards. Third-party providers may apply their own privacy policies when you choose to visit an external offer.
